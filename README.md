Cement Plant Operations & Cost Audit Dashboard:

Hello! As an aspiring Data Analyst, I built this project using Excel and Power BI to analyze daily operations, transport bills, and customer credit for a cement manufacturing plant.

What I Found & Recommended:

1. Customer Credit Limit Issue:
What I noticed: 
Customers owed ₹24.09M, which was ₹3.09M higher than our safe limit of ₹21.0M. Two major buyers made up most of this overdue balance.

My suggestion: 
Set up an automatic stop in the billing system so no new orders go out once a customer crosses 100% of their credit limit.

2. Extra Transport Charges:

What I noticed: 
Checking truck bills against our agreed contract rates caught ₹65,341 in extra charges across 36 trips. Some transporters were adding unapproved rate increases and extra waiting fees.

My suggestion: 
Check three things before paying any transporter: the agreed contract rate, the weighbridge weight slip, and the factory gate entry/exit time.

3. High Electricity Usage:

What I noticed: 
Power usage went up to 86.28 kWh/MT (above our target of 80 kWh/MT). When machines broke down for up to 8.5 hours a day, the kiln cooled down and needed extra electricity to heat back up.

My suggestion: 
Set an early alert for the maintenance team whenever power usage goes above 83 kWh/MT for two days in a row.

What Tools I Used

Microsoft Excel: Checked raw data files, matched contract rates, and looked for billing mistakes.

Power BI: Connected different data tables together, wrote basic DAX formulas, and created this two-page visual report.
